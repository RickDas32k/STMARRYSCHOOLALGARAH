On campus

Facilities

St. Mary's School believes in the overall development of every student, balancing academics, extracurricular activities and sport within the campus.

On campus

Facilities

Digital classrooms & labs

A composite science laboratory, a maths lab and a computer lab with a good number of systems, alongside digital classrooms that teach through audio-visual aids.

Library

An open-access library managed by a professional librarian, with subject reference books, CBSE exam materials and a range of periodicals and magazines.

Games & sports

Trained games and physical education teachers guide students in football, cricket, volleyball, chess, carrom, table tennis and badminton, alongside regular fitness drills.

Transport

School transport helps students from across the Paiyong valley reach the campus safely for the school day.

Dance & music

Dedicated sessions give students room to explore dance and music alongside their academic studies.

Science lab

A composite laboratory gives students hands-on experience alongside their science curriculum.
