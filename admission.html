Admission for 2027-2028

St. Mary's School admits students from Nursery to Class X, English medium, co-educational, irrespective of caste, class, race or religion. Enquiries for the 2027-2028 session are open now.

Admission enquiries are welcomed through the school office. Bring your child's birth certificate, previous school records (for transfers) and passport-size photographs when you visit, and our staff will guide you through the 2027-28 session's process, seat availability and fee schedule.

As a non-profit institution serving the Paiyong valley, the school keeps its process straightforward and its doors open to families across the community.

Minimum age for admission

Under the National Education Policy 2020, a child must have completed 6 years of age to enter Class I. Age is counted as on 31 March of the year the session begins.

  1. Nursery 3+ Completed 3 years
  2. LKG 4+ Completed 4 years
  3. UKG 5+ Completed 5 years
  4. Class I 6+ Completed 6 years, NEP 2020 norm

For the 2027-28 session, a child joining Class I must be born on or before 31 March 2021.

CBSE does not set one single all-India age rule. For admission up to Class VIII, affiliated schools follow the rules of their State or Union Territory, and the exact cut-off date can vary. Families should confirm their child's eligibility with the school office before applying.

For admission to a class above Class I, the child's age should be the Class I minimum plus one year for each year of schooling in between.
